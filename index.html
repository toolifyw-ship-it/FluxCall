FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the refund policy work?

Refunds are limited to 10 out of every 100 users on a first-come, first-served basis within the 7-day window. One refund per email AND per device. This ensures fair usage and prevents abuse of our refund system.

Can I get multiple free trials?

No. Our anti-abuse system tracks both email AND device. One free trial per email address and per device. Multiple accounts from the same device or email are automatically blocked to prevent misuse.

What happens when my plan expires?

You receive reminders 3 days before expiry. After expiry, service is paused until renewal. Your data is retained for 30 days. Upgrade anytime to restore full access instantly.

How many countries does FluxCall support?

Is FluxCall DPDP compliant?
